# Riparian wetland

Parent: [[Processes]]

The 3D land is a North American riparian wetland: river with flow, mud and soil banks, reeds, shrubs, grass, rocks, and mixed deciduous trees. Open channel is left for a dam.

River, plants, and colony follow the live height field and sea level.
