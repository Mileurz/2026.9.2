# Wildlife

Parent: [[Processes]]

Quiet wetland wildlife — ducks, small birds, frogs, fish, insects — appears as the pond and marsh grow. They drift and perch. No game AI.

The Season tag reports Wetland Wildlife as Sparse, Low, Moderate, or High. The wildlife slider changes how much you see.
