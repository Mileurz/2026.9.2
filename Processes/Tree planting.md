# Tree planting

Parent: [[Processes]]

On the Tree tab: click land to plant. Place, Delete, Clear. Catalog includes paper, wood, branch trees, and rock.

Scatter on land fills plantable ground with the selected kind. Placement is irregular (clumps and gaps), not a grid. Trees do not plant in river, sea, or ponds, and not on cliffs.

Planted trees also feed the colony as [[Processes/Tree resource]].
