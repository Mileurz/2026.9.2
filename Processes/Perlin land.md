# Perlin land

Parent: [[Processes]]

Height is Perlin fBm: n = Σ aⁱ · perlin(lⁱ f x, lⁱ f y), then sea, plains, and ridged(1 − |n|) from that same n.

Apply height field to the 3D plane. Default patch is forest-grove scale: smaller and lower, so a colony can sit in woodland rather than a mountain range.

Waterline foam is a thin, broken speckle, not a solid white band.
