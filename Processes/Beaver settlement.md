# Beaver settlement

Parent: [[Processes]]

Two adults (B-01, B-02) share a riverside home. They walk and swim the shore. Click a beaver for a record panel and an optional follow camera.

The year starts in autumn: cut wood, raise a dam if the reach is narrow, cache food. Not every reach gets a dam.

Restart colony restores this founding pair.
