# Dam

Parent: [[Processes]]

If the channel near home is narrow and wet enough, the pair builds a dam. Material goes in one piece at a time: branches, logs, mud, stones, vegetation. The wall grows longer, thicker, taller, and denser, and sits above the water.

A wide or poor reach stays bank living: forage, no dam. That is a simplified habitat rule, not a full engineering model.

The Water tag reports dam length, height, material, and construction progress.
