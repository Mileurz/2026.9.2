# Beaver movement

Parent: [[Processes]]

Beavers swim the channel as the main path, cut on the bank, and haul wood through the water. Kits stay by the lodge. Adults rest there in winter.

They keep space so they do not overlap. Motion is capped so high simulation speed does not teleport them.
