# Territory

Parent: [[Processes]]

Territory is an irregular overlay along shore, pond, river, food, and beaver routes — not a circle. Show Territory on the toolbar.

When the natal group is crowded, older offspring can leave along the waterway, avoid the home range, and look for trees and a possible dam or lodge site. A second settlement can form later if the habitat allows it.
