# Land fit

Parent: [[Processes]]

When you change the height model (sea, noise, Apply), the river is traced again. Home, dam, and lodge reseat on the current channel and bank. Resident beavers move with that home.

On the Colony tab the land does not keep scrolling, so the colony stays in view.
