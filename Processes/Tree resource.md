# Tree resource

Parent: [[Processes]]

Beavers pick one tree, gnaw it at the base, and stay with it. The tree falls in stages: gnawed, falling, fallen, stump, branches on the ground.

Willow, aspen, and poplar are preferred food. Standing forage limits how large the family can grow.

The UI reports available branches, wood collected, trees remaining, trees cut, and dead trees.
