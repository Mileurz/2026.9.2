# Population

Parent: [[Processes]]

The founding pair ages through kit, yearling, subadult, and adult. They mate in winter. Kits arrive in spring after about 106 days. First litter is usually 2–4. Yearlings stay and help.

The natal group stays small (about 8 at home, 10 in all). Growth needs a den (dam or lodge started) and enough forage trees. It does not increase forever.

Click a beaver for kinship. The Beavers tag reports population by stage. Breeding and activity sliders change the rate.
