# Time and season

Parent: [[Processes]]

Colony time is a simulation clock, not the render FPS. Pause, 1×, 5×, 20×, 100×. The UI shows Year, Month, and Day. Default demo speed is 5×.

The year starts in early autumn. Seasons blend: vegetation, foliage colour, flow, snow, pond ice.

Beaver year (simplified):

- Autumn — cut, dam, lodge, cache
- Winter — rest in the lodge, mate
- Spring — kits after about 106 days; older young may leave
- Summer — feed and maintain

Date drives aging, reproduction, stump regrowth, succession, and seasons. Pause freezes simulation time.
