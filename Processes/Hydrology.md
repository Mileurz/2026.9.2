# Hydrology

Parent: [[Processes]]

Dam growth holds water. Upstream level rises gradually. The pond spreads over low ground. The shoreline moves. Downstream stays lower with weaker current.

This is a simplified wetland model — not measured hydrology or CFD. The Time tag on the site says so.

The Water tag reports water level, pond area, and flooded area. Pond rise and flow can be adjusted.
