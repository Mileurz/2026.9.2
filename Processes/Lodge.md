# Lodge

Parent: [[Processes]]

After the dam starts, the pair builds a lodge in the pond: small branch pile → early lodge → established lodge → mature lodge. Sticks, branches, logs, mud, and reeds. Submerged entrance, dry living chamber. The lodge becomes home.

The Beavers tag reports lodge stage, material, and size.
