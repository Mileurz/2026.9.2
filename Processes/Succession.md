# Succession

Parent: [[Processes]]

As the pond rises, the land changes slowly. Trees stress, die, and become standing or fallen deadwood. Reeds, cattails, sedges, grasses, and aquatic plants fill wet ground. Old shorelines, paths, stumps, and cut trees remain.

The Forest and Season tags report wetland area, trees remaining, trees cut, dead trees, and flooded forest area. Tree regrowth and flood stress can be adjusted.
